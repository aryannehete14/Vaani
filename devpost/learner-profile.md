# Learner Profile

## Initial Idea
A "Voice-to-Website AI Agent" for small retail shop owners (bakeries, boutiques, kirana stores): the owner speaks or types a description of their shop and gets a responsive storefront with a product catalog, search, hours/open status, contact and location, and WhatsApp ordering. Responds to the hackathon problem statement "Why can't shop owners create websites without coding knowledge?"

## Demonstrated Technical and Agent Experience
Not established from conversation. The learner supplied a detailed technical brief (React/Vite, Tailwind, Framer Motion, Express or FastAPI, LLM structured output, SQLite) and asked the agent to execute the build end to end.

## Planning-Workflow Experience
Familiar with the plan-first workflow at the brief level: requested scope.md, prd.md and spec.md before code, with specific required sections (Gherkin stories, OpenAPI, schema).

## Desired Learning Outcome
Not established. Stated goal: a winning, production-ready, open-source hackathon submission that meets all Devpost requirements.

## Interests, Inspirations, and Goals
Design quality is the top judging priority (first tie-breaker): polished, accessible, mobile-first UI with micro-interactions. Emphasis on realistic retail impact (WhatsApp ordering, open/closed status, search) and on the voice-to-storefront pipeline as the innovation.

## Collaboration and Communication Preferences
Prefers the agent to execute in a strict step order with minimal back-and-forth.

## Areas Where the Learner Wants Ownership
Not established. Planning documents were drafted from the learner's brief and are marked `status: draft` pending the learner's review.
